FAQ

A few answers
before the call.

If it still feels like a fit after this section, that’s a good sign.

What does a typical project timeline look like?
+
Most identity or motion-led projects run between 3–6 weeks depending on scope. Smaller content packs can be turned around faster once the system is defined.
Can you work with our in-house team or agency?
+
Yes. A lot of work happens alongside internal teams — I can plug in as a dedicated design/motion arm or help define the system they execute on.
Do you handle scripting and copy as well?
+
For motion and launch films, yes — I usually help shape the narrative, script and flow, then we lock visuals and timing together. For smaller projects, you provide the copy.
What’s the best way to start?
+
A short call with a clear brief and a single decision-maker on your side. From there we map scope, budget and timeline in one page.
