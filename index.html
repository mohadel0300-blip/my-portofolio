03 / ABOUT

The path.

I studied Mining Engineering, but the part of work that kept pulling me in was visual communication. I began in news and video, then moved gradually into graphic design. It wasn’t a sudden career switch; it was the direction my work had already been taking.

I’m comfortable with both kinds of work: the quick piece that has to go out today, and the longer project that needs a visual system people can keep using. I care about clarity, consistency and making the design useful — not just attractive.
