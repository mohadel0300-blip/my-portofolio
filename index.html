FAQ

What does a typical project timeline look like?
Most identity or motion-led projects run between 3–6 weeks depending on scope. Smaller content packs can be turned around faster.
Can you work with our in-house team?
Yes. I often plug in next to internal teams — either as a dedicated design/motion arm or to define the system they execute on.
Do you handle scripting?
For motion and launch films, yes. I usually help shape the narrative, script and flow before locking visuals.
How to start?
A short call with a clear brief. From there we map scope, budget and timeline in one page.
