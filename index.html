About Mohamed

A designer who connects visuals, motion and strategy – turning complex products into clear stories.

I'm a Graphic & Motion Designer with more than five years of experience in visual identity, social media systems, product videos, packaging and political / social awareness campaigns.

I’ve worked with TV channels, medical brands, detector manufacturers, jewelry brands and studios across Turkey, Saudi Arabia and the region, producing daily content that has to be clear, fast and on-brand.

I also studied Mining Engineering at Muğla Sıtkı Koçman University (2020 – 2025), which gave me an analytical and structured way of thinking in my design process.
